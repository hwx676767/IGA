Testimony
I am ??
I woke up in the shelter with no memory of who I was or how I got there. My terminal still functions, but my identity login has been completely erased, leaving me unable to access any personal records or information. Everything I once knew about myself seems to have disappeared along with my identity.

Shelter’s Rules
Carol explains that one of the shelter’s most important rules is that everyone must use aliases when speaking to one another. No real names, personal records, or identifying information are to be shared unless absolutely necessary. And the use of any smart electronic products is prohibited.

Systemed Society
My terminal has no active identity login, and in a society built entirely around Synapse. I cannot purchase anything, access public services, or use public transportation, because every transaction and movement requires a verified identity. Even basic necessities are tied to the system, making it impossible for me to function normally outside the shelter. 
Mysterious Diary
Even though the diary appears to belong to a man you do not recognize. Carol insists that the damaged diary was dropped by you; however, she cannot explain how she knows this. She refuses to give it to anyone else and maintains that it should be in your hands. 

The Falcon

Mentioned in the diary, was a club formed by various figures from different sectors of the system twelve years ago.


Trouble Classification

The Administration Bureau categorizes terminal services into four types: A D R U Z, A for new users, D for damage and malfunctions, R for renewal, U for upgrade, Z for uncategorized issues. And the numbers are individual serial numbers that make up the number on the receipt.


Scanner

Scanning the receipt using the administor’s terminal will provide more personal information about the service applicant.


Gig life

Joanne owns no terminal and survives by taking whatever odd jobs she can find, while occasionally helping Carol with the church’s daily affairs. She has lived in the shelter for a long time, claiming that it is the only place where she feels relatively safe.

Anniversary Gift box
The candy wrapper bearing the tenth anniversary label was specially commissioned for the System Administration. According to the clerk, Wayne, it was part of a commemorative gift box distributed to Bureau employees.
