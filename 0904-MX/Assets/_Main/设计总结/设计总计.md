# Mahou Circle — Design Document

**Project Type:** Narrative Investigation / Web-Browsing Simulation  
**Role:** Lead Designer (Narrative, Systems, Content — independent)  
**Status:** Playable prototype available (art & engineering developed separately)  
**Prepared for:** USC Interactive Media & Games (IMGD) — Graduate Application Portfolio

---

## 1. Elevator Pitch

*Mahou Circle* is a narrative investigation game in which the player explores a abandoned fan forum once devoted to magical-girl fandom. What begins as casual browsing gradually reveals a layered conspiracy: a medical institution exploited vulnerable teenage users, harvested them through a disguised community platform, and abandoned the site once its purpose was fulfilled.

The player is not a hero who saves anyone. They are a curious outsider reconstructing history from broken links, contradictory announcements, and hidden internal reports — and the final revelation is that understanding the truth changes nothing. The institution still operates.

**Core experience:** Curiosity → suspicion → active investigation → systemic understanding → unsettling closure.

---

## 2. Design Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Browsing as gameplay** | Reading, navigating, and searching a simulated website *is* the primary interaction — not a delivery mechanism for cutscenes. |
| **Layered truth** | Information exists on three distinct tiers (public forum → corporate facade → internal reports), each with its own voice, aesthetics, and level of concealment. |
| **Player-initiated discovery** | Anomalies (dead links, mismatched admin accounts, recurring locations) must be noticed by the player; the game rewards attention, not reflex. |
| **Chronological literacy** | Progress depends on reading posts in temporal context — tone shifts from fandom enthusiasm to mutual aid to cult-like tutorialization are themselves clues. |
| **Investigation without agency** | The player gains knowledge but not power. This deliberate futility is a thematic and mechanical choice, not a design failure. |

These pillars align with USC IMGD's emphasis on **player experience design**, **interactive storytelling**, and **games as cultural commentary**.

## 3. Genre & Reference Context

**Primary genre:** Environmental narrative / alt-game investigation  
**Interaction model:** Non-linear web exploration with keyword search gating

**Conversational references (not clones):**

- *Hypnospace Outlaw* — browsing a simulated internet as core loop; corporate malfeasance hidden behind friendly UI
- *Her Story* / *Immortality* — fragment-based reconstruction of events through search and comparison
- *Analogue: A Hate Story* — forum archaeology revealing systemic harm to women in online spaces
- *Return of the Obra Dinn* — deduction from incomplete records; the player as historian, not protagonist

**Differentiation:** *Mahou Circle* grounds its horror in a specific subcultural context (magical-girl fandom as adolescent coping mechanism) and maps the exploitation pipeline across three synchronized web properties — a fan community, a medical brand site, and an internal research dashboard — rather than a single archive.

## 4. World & Narrative Overview

### 4.1 Premise

Rumors persist that users of a once-popular website called **Mahou Circle** "disappeared" in real life. The site still exists online, partially maintained, partially gutted. The player, motivated by urban-legend curiosity, decides to investigate what happened.

### 4.2 Setting Timeline (2013–2015)

| Period | Forum State | Underlying Reality |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **2013 H1** | Healthy magical-girl fan community; fan art, character discussions, first offline meetup plans | Normal subcultural space |
| **2013 H2** | Support posts increase; "magic" becomes emotional survival language | Vulnerable users self-select into visible distress |
| **2014 Q1–Q2** | "Magical girl has descended" news triggers identity fusion; tutorial posts emerge | External event catalyzes delusion spiral |
| **2014 Q3** | Drug-assisted "perception stabilization" guides circulate; offline events become monthly and selective | Predatory actors + institution begin coordinated recruitment |
| **2014 Q4** | Original admin warns against unverified info; investigates offline, then vanishes | Admin becomes liability to operation |
| **2015 Q1** | New admin takes over; account naming patterns match earlier bad actors; "神隐" (spiritual disappearance) reframed as honor | Hostile takeover complete |
| **2015 Q2** | Offline events permanently cancelled; cleanup announcement admits broken links; site enters managed decay | Institution exits; internal staff deliberately leave traces |

### 4.3 The Institution — ReNova Aesthetic Institute

ReNova presents publicly as a cutting-edge regenerative aesthetics clinic ("Youth Optimization / Cellular Renewal / Precision Enhancement"). Internally, it conducts Phase II–III protocols on adolescent "subjects" selected for:

- Limited external dependency  
- Reduced social interference  
- High adaptability under isolated conditions  

Selection data is harvested from the forum's Support and Talk sections. Offline "同好交流会" (fan meetups) are held inside ReNova's **Central Exchange Hall** — the same location referenced across public event posts and internal field-operation logs.

After extraction cycles, subjects are **returned** to families under a cover story of "national confidential training programs," with stipends to suppress inquiry. Returned subjects exhibit behavioral compliance — interpreted by families as successful correction.

### 4.4 Ending State

The player unlocks all accessible pages and understands the full pipeline: forum → screening → abduction → experimentation → return → site abandonment. The institution is never confronted. The player's browser closes on an empty, link-broken shell — a deliberate mirror of real-world investigative futility.

## 5. Three-Layer Information Architecture

This is the project's central systems design contribution.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  LAYER 1: Mahou Circle (Public Forum)                   │
│  Voice: Casual, fandom-native, gradually distressed     │
│  Player sees: Posts, announcements, broken links        │
│  Reveals: Surface community history, tone shift,        │
│           suspicious admin transition                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LAYER 2: ReNova Public Website                         │
│  Voice: Clinical, aspirational, corporate-clean         │
│  Player sees: Marketing copy, team bios, contact gate   │
│  Reveals: Terminology overlap with internal logs;       │
│           location match with forum event posts         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  LAYER 3: ReNova Internal Staff Portal                  │
│  Voice: Bureaucratic, euphemistic, data-driven          │
│  Player sees: Search-gated reports (12 keyword entries)   │
│  Reveals: Selection criteria, compliance protocols,     │
│           return procedures, archive clearance          │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Design intent:** Each layer uses a different rhetorical register. The player learns to cross-reference vocabulary — when "Behavioral Optimization" on the internal portal echoes Support-section coping language on the forum, the connection becomes undeniable without explicit exposition.

## 6. Core Gameplay Loop

```
Browse open pages
      ↓
Encounter anomaly (dead link / contradictory admin / recurring location)
      ↓
Shift from reader → investigator
      ↓
Use in-site search with keywords discovered in content
      ↓
Unlock hidden pages & internal reports
      ↓
Compare timestamps → reconstruct chronological transformation
      ↓
Access all unlockable pages → form systemic conclusion
```

**Win condition:** Visit every unlockable page and comprehend the full institutional pipeline.  
**No fail state:** The game does not punish wrong searches; it rewards persistent attention. Wrong keywords simply return nothing — mirroring real research dead ends.

---

## 7. Player Journey — Seven Phases

| Phase | Name | Player Action | Design Intent |
|-------|------|---------------|---------------|
| **I** | Information Reception | Read homepage, site intro, latest admin notice about "ongoing cleanup" | Establish false sense of post-crisis normalcy |
| **II** | Surface Stability | Browse open sections (Fan Art, Talk, Support, QnA); note structure and timestamps | Teach forum literacy; content appears moderated |
| **III** | First Fracture | Attempt to access referenced-but-dead links and early posts | Trigger suspicion: deletions are selective, not natural decay |
| **IV** | Investigation Begins | Use in-site search when hyperlinks fail | Core mechanic activation; player earns information |
| **V** | Pattern Recognition | Compare search results chronologically; notice recurring account naming conventions | Player deduces managed takeover, not organic community collapse |
| **VI** | Institutional Contour | Unlock ReNova public site + internal portal pages | Cross-site evidence convergence (location, terminology, selection logic) |
| **VII** | Conclusion | Access final deletion rationale and incomplete archive state | Player understands abandonment as intentional; no cathartic resolution |

**Key turning point (Phase III → IV):** The moment the player realizes missing content was *removed*, not lost. This converts passive reading into active inquiry — the foundational IMGD-relevant shift from consumption to interaction.

## 8. Content Design — Mahou Circle Forum

### 8.1 Section Structure

| Section | Function | Narrative Role |
|---------|----------|----------------|
| **Home** | Landing, welcome copy | Sets nostalgic, sincere tone |
| **Announcements** | Admin posts (chronological) | Primary timeline anchor; voice changes track admin identity shift |
| **Fan Art** | Creative sharing | Establishes healthy origin state |
| **Talk** | General discussion | Introduces delusion content, tutorial posts, location leaks via comments |
| **Support** | Anonymous emotional posts | Screening data source; shows institution's target profile |
| **QnA** | User questions to admin | Surfaces community anxiety ("神隐", "觉醒", selection criteria) |

### 8.2 Announcement Arc (Key Beats)

- **2013/9/30** — First offline meetup invitation; warm, community-driven tone  
- **2014/7/1** — Original admin cancels July event due to "concerning information"; warns about stranger DMs  
- **2014/9/15** — Admin delegates management to "trusted members" (foreshadows takeover)  
- **2014/9/23** — New admin reinstates monthly offline events with **invitation-only selection**  
- **2015/4/1** — All offline activity permanently suspended  
- **2015/6/28** — Current admin: "historical links temporarily inaccessible; cleanup ongoing"  

### 8.3 Account Design Patterns

Repeated avatars (G, H, I, J, etc.) serve as continuity anchors:

- **Avatar G** — Early commenter who normalizes suspicious activity ("nothing special, same as before")  
- **Avatar H** — Connects event location to ReNova's former address; posts fragmented URL as puzzle  
- **Avatar I** — Publishes "stabilization method" guide with drug references and DM gatekeeping  
- **Avatar J** — Asks recruitment-oriented questions ("how to be chosen by spirits")  

Naming and posting patterns between these accounts and the post-takeover admin account are intentionally similar — a discoverable clue that management and exploitation share origin.

## 9. Search System — Internal Portal Keywords

The internal staff portal requires keyword search. Twelve terms map to report entries, each revealing one facet of the operation:

| Keyword | Report Title | Narrative Function |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|
| `regenerative protocol` | Phase II Overview | Confirms medical experimentation framing |
| `cellular stability` | CSI Internal Report | Subject scoring and prioritization logic |
| `behavioral optimization` | BO Guidelines | Links clinical language to forum coping discourse |
| `subject selection` | Selection Criteria | Explicit target profile (isolated, low social dependency) |
| `offline session` | Session Arrangement | Confirms physical abduction pathway |
| `compliance` | Compliance Monitoring | Describes post-procedure behavioral control |
| `phase transition` | Phase Transition Log | Documents identity alteration in subjects |
| `return protocol` | Return Protocol Summary | Explains family-facing cover story |
| `data source` | Data Acquisition Channels | Confirms forum as harvesting platform |
| `deviation` | Deviation Case Review | Acknowledges failed subjects; systemic risk management |
| `archive clearance` | Archive Clearance Notice | Explains why site was not fully deleted |
| `anomaly` | Anomaly Flag | Meta-commentary: player activity detected |

**Design rationale:** Keywords are embedded in public-facing content before the internal portal is accessible. The player must *collect vocabulary* from Layer 1 and 2 before Layer 3 yields meaning — a gated literacy progression, not a key-collection fetch quest.

## 10. Thematic Framework

### Primary Themes

1. **Subculture as survival infrastructure** — Magical-girl imagery functions not as escapist fantasy alone, but as the only language available for adolescent distress.  
2. **Platform complicity** — A community site becomes a sorting mechanism; moderation failure is indistinguishable from intentional capture.  
3. **Institutional euphemism** — "Youth Optimization" / "Behavioral Stabilization" / "Return Protocol" sanitize violence through professional vocabulary.  
4. **Investigative futility** — Knowledge without enforcement power; the horror is systemic persistence, not a resolved case.  
5. **Urban legend as recruitment funnel** — Deliberate mystification of the site post-operation attracts new curious visitors — the player themselves replicate this cycle.

### Responsible Framing (Design Stance)

Sensitive content (minors, drug induction, medical exploitation) is presented through **documentary detachment** — forum posts, corporate copy, internal reports — never through sensationalized depiction of harm. The design positions the player as witness and analyst, not voyeur. Critique targets institutional and predatory structures, not victims.

This framing supports USC's interest in **games that engage with social reality** while maintaining ethical design boundaries.
