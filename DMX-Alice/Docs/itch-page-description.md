# itch.io Description（可直接粘贴）

> **用法**  
> 1. 打开 itch 项目编辑页 → **Description**  
> 2. 优先粘贴下方「中文版」或「英文版」整段  
> 3. itch 支持 Markdown；颜色部分用了 HTML `<span>`，在 itch 预览里一般可用  
> 4. 封面/截图请在页面 **Uploads / Cover** 单独上传，文案里的图片链接上传后可再替换

## 中文版（推荐粘贴）

```html
<h2><span style="color:#e8c56a;">DMX-Alice</span> — 聚光灯之外，戏也要开演</h2>

<p><strong>试镜失败了。</strong>观众在笑，灯光也不再为你停留。<br>
但演出并不会因此停下来——幕布升起之前，另一处舞台早已热闹起来：<strong>后台</strong>。</p>

<p>在这里，灵感会逃走，道具要亲手做，布景要在节奏里对位。<br>
你不是主角，却可能是让这一晚成立的人。</p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">关于本作</span></h3>

<p><em>DMX-Alice</em> 是一款短篇叙事互动体验：从落选的演员，变成剧组后台的一员。<br>
新剧《<strong>Moonlit Tryst</strong>（月下幽会）》需要布景与道具——而你的工作，是把散落的念头收成可用的舞台。</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>叙事驱动</strong>：对话推进故事，认识导演 Sam、主演 Roxy 等角色</li>
  <li><strong>抓灵感</strong>：在思绪里分辨「有用」与「噪音」</li>
  <li><strong>动手制作</strong>：用工具完成道具</li>
  <li><strong>舞台对位</strong>：在演出节奏中布置场景</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">你将经历</span></h3>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>01 · Brainstorm / 抓灵感</strong></span><br>
破冰小游戏教会你：点子来得快，有的溜走，有的只是杂音。<br>
先点掉遮挡视线的干扰，再选出正确选项；随后在飘动的关键词里，留下能撑起《月下幽会》的那些——城堡、烛火、长桌、羊皮卷……</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>02 · Prop Making / 道具制作</strong></span><br>
灵感落地成物。拖拽工具，把设定做成能上台的道具。</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>03 · Set Dressing / 布景与演出</strong></span><br>
月色、树影、长桌与信物——在节奏里摆好这一晚的位置。</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>04 · Ending / 收束</strong></span><br>
聚光灯下是别人的名字；幕后，是你留下的痕迹。</p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">操作</span></h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>鼠标左键</strong>：推进对话、选择选项、点击弹幕/干扰项、拖拽道具</li>
  <li><strong>选项关</strong>：按钮固定在画面中，请在倒计时内点选；有深色干扰时先点掉再选答案</li>
  <li><strong>关键词关</strong>：点击飘过的灵感词（有用留下，噪音清掉）</li>
</ul>

<p><em>建议游玩分辨率：1920×1080（或接近的横屏窗口）</em></p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">游玩时长 &amp; 平台</span></h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>时长</strong>：约一局短篇（数分钟至十余分钟，视阅读速度而定）</li>
  <li><strong>平台</strong>：Windows 下载版（推荐）</li>
  <li><strong>引擎</strong>：Unity 2022.3</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">制作说明</span></h3>

<p>学生项目 / 课程作品。<br>
故事与玩法围绕「落选之后，如何在幕后把一出戏撑起来」展开——<strong>不是成为明星，而是学会留下有用的那一部分。</strong></p>

<p>若你喜欢短叙事、后台视角、轻量小玩法，欢迎试玩与反馈。</p>

<hr>

<p style="color:#9aa0a6;"><em>Thank you for playing. The curtain rises either way.</em></p>
```

## 英文版（面向国际页 / 双语可并贴）

```html
<h2><span style="color:#e8c56a;">DMX-Alice</span> — The show goes on backstage</h2>

<p><strong>You failed the audition.</strong> The audience snickers. The spotlight moves on.<br>
But a play is not only what happens downstage. Long before the curtain rises, another stage is already alive — <strong>backstage</strong>.</p>

<p>Ideas flee. Some are useful; some are just noise. Props must be made. The set must land on cue.<br>
You may not stand in the lights — but you can hold the night together.</p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">About</span></h3>

<p><em>DMX-Alice</em> is a short narrative experience about joining a school musical crew after a failed acting audition.<br>
The new show is <strong>Moonlit Tryst</strong> — a meeting at the treeline, a token at the table. Your job is to catch what fits, clear what doesn’t, and build the night.</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Story-led</strong> — dialogue with Sam, Roxy, and the crew</li>
  <li><strong>Brainstorm</strong> — separate useful thoughts from noise</li>
  <li><strong>Prop making</strong> — craft with tools</li>
  <li><strong>Stage dressing</strong> — place the scene to the rhythm of the show</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">Chapters</span></h3>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>01 · Brainstorm</strong></span><br>
An ice-breaker teaches the rule: ideas come fast; keep what’s worth keeping.<br>
Clear dark interference, pick the right answer under a timer, then catch keyword thoughts that can hold <em>Moonlit Tryst</em> — castle, candlelight, long table, parchment…</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>02 · Prop Making</strong></span><br>
Turn ideas into objects. Drag tools. Finish what the stage needs.</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>03 · Set Dressing</strong></span><br>
Moonlight, leaves, table, token — place them where the scene can breathe.</p>

<p><span style="color:#e8c56a;"><strong>04 · Ending</strong></span><br>
Someone else takes the bow. You left the bones of the night.</p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">Controls</span></h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Left click</strong> — advance dialogue, choose options, clear noise, catch keywords, drag tools/props</li>
  <li><strong>Choice rounds</strong> — stationary buttons; clear dark cards first when they cover answers</li>
  <li><strong>Keyword round</strong> — click floating thoughts (keep the helpful ones)</li>
</ul>

<p><em>Best played around 1920×1080 landscape.</em></p>

<hr>

<h3><span style="color:#7ec8e3;">Details</span></h3>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Length</strong> — a short session</li>
  <li><strong>Platform</strong> — Windows download</li>
  <li><strong>Engine</strong> — Unity 2022.3</li>
  <li><strong>Status</strong> — student / class project</li>
</ul>

<hr>

<p style="color:#9aa0a6;"><em>Thank you for playing. The curtain rises either way.</em></p>
```
