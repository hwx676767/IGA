   - BrainstormDanmakuButton.cs -> Assets/_Main/Scripts/
   - BrainstormThought.cs -> Assets/_Main/Scripts/
   - DanmakuRevealPlaceholder.cs -> Assets/_Main/Scripts/

3. Return to Unity and let it recompile.
