
A group of people fleeing from war discovered an island with remnants of human civilization and built a self-sustaining society there.

Years later, as society developed, natural resources became scarce, and the remaining "tech ruins" gradually stopped working, unable to sustain the island's operations.

To cope, the government passed a law stating that those who could no longer contribute to society (such as the elderly or people with disabilities) must go to the government office for "suicide services."

If you want to keep living, you must endure inhumane treatment and contribute value to society.

You are a registrar at the "suicide service" office, passively accepting this reality.

Then one day, after a drunken fall from a platform, you lose one of your legs...
